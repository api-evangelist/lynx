# Lynx (Lynx FinHealth)

> Lynx FinHealth is a healthcare fintech platform providing embedded banking, payments, benefits, and e-commerce infrastructure for health plans, providers, and digital health companies. Its API-first and white-label platform powers consumer-directed health accounts (HSA/FSA), ICHRA administration, directed/filtered spend accounts, instant card issuance with merchant/SKU controls, supplemental benefits, pharmacy access, rewards, and a QME e-commerce marketplace. SOC 2 and HITRUST certified.
