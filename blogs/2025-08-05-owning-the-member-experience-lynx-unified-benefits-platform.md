A New Era for Health Plan Member Experiences Healthcare members expect seamless, intuitive experiences, similar to those in retail or banking—a single place to manage their benefits, access services, and engage with their health plan. Legacy systems, with their fragmented vendor networks, siloed platforms, and disconnected tools have left members bouncing between call centers, benefit portals, and apps just to access what they need, leading to member frustration and operational inefficiencies.
